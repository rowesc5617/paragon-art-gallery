Membership

Become a member of The Paragon Art Gallery to enjoy unlimited annual admission, discounts on classes and supplies, and invitations to exclusive preview events.

Individual Membership

Unlimited admission for one named adult for a full year, 10% discount on classes and supplies, and invitations to member-only exhibition previews.

Price: $60.00 / year

Dual Membership

Unlimited admission for two adults living in the same household, plus two complimentary guest passes and 10% off gallery store purchases.

Price: $100.00 / year

Family Membership

Unlimited admission for two adults and up to four children under 18, 15% discount on all family art classes, and priority event registration.

Price: $140.00 / year

Patron Circle

All Family benefits plus free admission to paid special workshops, private curator tours, and recognition on our gallery benefactor wall.

Price: $250.00 / year
